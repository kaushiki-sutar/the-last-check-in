The Last Check-In · Chapter One

The Empty Reception

The entrance clicks shut behind you. A lamp flickers above the counter. Everything is covered in dust—except an envelope.

Your objective: Find the emergency exit key.

Exit key: Not found

Beyond the counter stands a door marked “Emergency Exit.”

Look a little closer

Inspect object

Somewhere in this reception, the guests left you an answer.

The lock clicks open. Inside lies a brass key and a faded photograph.

The photograph shows this very reception. On the back, someone has written: “Our expected guest, 1926.”

The Last Check-In · Check-out complete

You checked out. The hotel remembers.

The brass key turns. You push the emergency door open and step into the cold night air. Behind you, the lock clicks one last time.

The rain has stopped. You turn to look back, but every window in the hotel is dark.

Something rustles inside your pocket. It is the photograph from the drawer. You don't remember taking it.

Now you notice the guest register in the photograph. Beneath the date, 1926, is your name— written in your own handwriting.

Somewhere inside the hotel, the reception bell rings.

You found the key and escaped the reception.
